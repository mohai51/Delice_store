HOW DÉLICE WAS BORN • 4 FRIENDS • 1 RELENTLESS QUEST

"Four Friends, Countless Sleepless Nights, and an Uncompromising Dream."

DÉLICE began around late-night university tables among four close friends—Sayod Sulayman Sukria, Mohaiminul Islam, Abdullah Basar Tarif, and Zayed Iqbal Munna. Frustrated by overpriced designer imports and harsh, short-lived imitations, we set out to craft genuine fine luxury fragrances in Bangladesh.

Crafting The Essence
Uncompromising Quality: Crafted exclusively with pure, high-concentration fine fragrance oils—zero cheap alcohol dilutions, zero shortcuts. True luxury, refined for lasting presence.

The Uncompromising Pursuit of Purity

We asked ourselves a simple question: Why can’t young people in Bangladesh experience genuine, world-class luxury fragrances at an honest price?

"We chose the harder road. In an industry flooded with diluted imitations, our greatest commitment was working only with 100% authentic, high-concentration perfume oils."

Bringing that vision to life demanded tireless dedication and months of trial. We spent night after night evaluating pure essences, rejecting countless substandard samples, and formulating dozens of prototypes. We mastered the maceration curve and precisely calibrated oil concentrations for Bangladesh’s tropical heat and daily rhythm—so every single drop radiates rich character, smooth evolution, and dependable longevity.

Every bottle of DÉLICE carries that promise: authentic fine fragrance oils, long-lasting performance, and honest luxury made accessible.
